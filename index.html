Clipper

Clipper turns long walking videos from my own YouTube channel into short vertical clips and posts them to my own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Page and TikTok accounts. I review and approve every clip before it is posted. Clipper is used only by me, for my own accounts. It is not offered to other creators.

How it works

  • finds strong moments in my long walking videos;
  • renders 9:16 clips that keep the original ambient sound;
  • I review every clip and approve or reject it;
  • approved clips are posted through each platform's official API.
