The data Clipper holds

  • Access tokens for the connected YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.
  • Clip files rendered from my videos.
  • Generated titles, descriptions, captions and hashtags.

How to request deletion

Email mrsmokeycapone@gmail.com to request deletion. The operator deletes everything listed above within 30 days and confirms by email.

How to revoke access on each platform yourself

  • Google: review and remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Instagram: remove the app in the connected apps or website permissions settings of your Instagram account.
  • Facebook: remove the app in the business integrations settings of your Facebook account.
  • TikTok: remove the app in the app permissions settings of your TikTok account.

The platform menus change over time, so these instructions name the settings rather than giving exact menu paths.
