Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-15

Who operates Clipper

Clipper is operated by SanpoSuru. Contact: mrsmokeycapone@gmail.com.

What Clipper accesses

YouTube

Clipper reads the public list of uploads on my channel with an API key. It uploads approved clips to my channel with the youtube.upload permission.

Instagram

Clipper reads my Instagram account ID, username and account type with instagram_business_basic. It publishes approved Reels to my account with instagram_business_content_publish.

Facebook

Clipper reads my Facebook Pages and their details with pages_show_list and pages_read_engagement. It publishes approved Reels to my Page with pages_manage_posts.

TikTok

Clipper reads my basic profile and nickname with user.info.basic, and my posting settings from TikTok's creator info. It posts approved videos with video.publish.

What Clipper stores and where

Access tokens are kept in a local environment file or secret store on my own machine, never in source control. Clip files and generated titles and descriptions stay on my machine, or on rented cloud compute only while they are being processed.

What Clipper does not do

  • It does not sell or share data.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics on this site.
  • It does not access anyone else's account.
  • It does not read messages or comments.

Retention

Tokens are kept until they are revoked or expire. Clip files are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Deleting data and revoking access

To revoke Clipper's access, remove it from each platform's connected apps or app permissions settings. For Google, review and remove access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete locally held data, delete the token file and the clip files on my machine.

Full deletion and revocation instructions are on the Data deletion page.
